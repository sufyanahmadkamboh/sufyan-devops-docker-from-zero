# Assets and licenses

Every sound and visual element of the two videos, where it comes from, and the terms it is used under.

**No external music or sound-effect files are used.** All music and sound effects are original: they are synthesised by
[`audio_assets.py`](audio_assets.py) from sine waves, filtered noise and simple envelopes (numpy/scipy). Nothing is
sampled, downloaded or derived from a recording, so there is no third-party audio license to comply with. They are part
of this repository and covered by its [MIT License](../LICENSE).

## The two copies

| File | Picture | Audio |
|---|---|---|
| `docker-from-zero-partN-full.mp4` | the edited video | narration + music + sound effects, mixed and normalised to -14 LUFS (true peak -1 dBTP) |
| `docker-from-zero-partN-silent.mp4` | **the identical video stream** (copied bit for bit from the same encode, `-c:v copy`) | none (no audio track) |

Because the silent copy reuses the exact encoded picture stream of the full copy, cuts, transitions, animations, text
and timing are identical by construction.
